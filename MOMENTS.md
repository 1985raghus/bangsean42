# Moments

My own words, as they happen. I say them in chat and they get added here;
nothing overwrites this file. `JOURNAL.md` quotes each moment against the run
it belongs to, with the numbers attached - that's the material for the weekly
Instagram post and, in November, the race report.

Format: `## YYYY-MM-DD`, then whatever I said.
